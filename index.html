BEFORE YOU HIT RECORD

A little preparation.

01 / THE EDIT

Make room for the story.

Target 25–35 minutes per finished episode. Record longer sessions as needed. Trim repeat travel, menu work, and failed attempts; keep every successful boss defeat on-screen.

Parts 1–4 describe your existing recordings provisionally. Keep your actual edit boundaries. Part 5 assumes Hollow Bastion’s opening defense is complete.

02 / THE BUILD

Train along the way.

Before Part 34, aim for level 7 Valor, Wisdom, Limit, Master, and Final Forms, plus shared Summon level 7. Equip the level-3 growth abilities for the Cavern.

Collect Chicken Little, Genie, Stitch’s Ukulele Charm, and Peter Pan’s Feather Charm. Budget additional preparation time between recordings.

03 / THE UNLOCKS

Finish each world.

Complete Pooh and Atlantica alongside both story visits to the Disney worlds. Finish the game in Part 30, create a completed-game save, and reload it before the Data campaign.

Collect Torn Pages from the Library, Oasis, Crystal Fissure, Throne Room, and Ruined Chamber. Complete all ordinary cups before the Paradox cups.

04 / THE SCOPE

Every boss. No busywork.

Five Absent Silhouettes, Sephiroth, Hades Paradox Cup, the full Cavern, all thirteen Data rematches, and Lingering Will are mandatory.

Ultima Weapon, synthesis completion, Mushroom XIII challenges, journal scores, and gummi collectibles are outside this roadmap. There is no save point in the unmodified KH2FM Garden: use the shortcut and save at the Postern.
