01 / THE RECORDING RULE

Every cutscene stays.

Exactly 45 episodes, with flexible length. Follow each episode’s chapter boundary, even when the video runs long. Estimates include cutscenes and commentary; the recording budget adds retry space. Cut only repetitive preparation, repeat failures, or dead travel if desired.

Parts 1–4 preserve your existing opening. Parts 5–30 finish the story, 31–43 clear every required optional challenge, and 44–45 watch the Days HD movie.

02 / FORMS & SUMMONS

Build the toolkit early.

Before Part 33, this route targets level 7 in Valor, Wisdom, Limit, Master, and Final, plus shared Summon level 7. Equip the level-3 growth abilities before the Cavern in Part 35. Grinding has its own off-camera budget.

Valor gains experience from hits; Wisdom from Heartless defeats; Limit from completed limit finishers; Master from Drive orbs; Final from Nobody defeats. Summons share experience as their gauge is consumed. Unlock Final after Roxas in Part 27.

03 / ESSENTIAL PICKUPS

Five pages. Four summons.

Torn Pages: Library in Part 9, Oasis in 14, Crystal Fissure in 17, Throne Room in 18, and Ruined Chamber in 21. Finish the book in Part 26.

Summons: Chicken Little in Part 7, Genie in 12, Stitch’s Ukulele Charm in 16, and Peter Pan’s Feather Charm in 20. Atlantica: early songs in 16, Ursula in 22, final song in 26.

04 / TOURNAMENT LADDER

Earn the last cup.

Ordinary cups: Pain and Panic in Part 10, Cerberus in 18, Titan in 24, Goddess of Fate in 29. Clear Space Paranoids II in 25. Finish all three preliminary Paradox cups in 33.

Part 34 covers all 50 Hades Paradox rounds, divided into five acts. Use earned ten-round checkpoints if the session needs a break. You need a victory, not the journal’s high-score target.

05 / CLEARED-GAME REQUIREMENTS

Save beyond the ending.

Complete all world stories, including Pooh and Atlantica, before the finale. Keep the full ending and credits in Part 30, create the completed-game save, and reload it. This establishes the unlocks for the complete postgame route.

Clear all five Silhouettes in Part 31. Open the Cavern shortcut in 35. The unmodified KH2FM Garden has no save point: leave through the Restoration Site shortcut and save at the Postern between Data victories.

06 / THE MOVIE EPILOGUE

Return to Roxas.

Defeat Lingering Will in Part 43 before beginning the watch. Part 44 starts the HD movie at the opening and ends after Day 193’s The Girl with the Sketch Book. Part 45 resumes at Day 194~: Axel Learns the Truth, then continues through Day 359 and all credits.

Keep the movie’s presented order, including its opening flash-forward and text bridges. Pause to discuss scenes; record the chapter bookmark. These are two long watch episodes, not additional playable missions.

07 / COMBAT LOADOUT

Make survival consistent.

Use Guard, Reflect, Cure, and accessible recovery items. Equip Second Chance and Once More when learned; their acquisition depends on your level and opening weapon choice. Keep movement abilities equipped and review both Sora and Riku before Xemnas.

Use naturally earned equipment such as Decisive Pumpkin. Ultima Weapon, synthesis completion, Mushroom XIII, journal medals, and gummi collectibles are outside this run.

08 / RECORDING HABITS

Let the scene land.

Begin with a short recap and loadout check. Let dialogue finish before analysis, keep every successful boss fight complete, and use travel breaks for commentary. For learning attempts, retain only moments that teach a new pattern.

Use each episode’s notes for timestamps, attempt lessons, and exact save or movie chapter. Export a progress backup before changing browsers or devices.
